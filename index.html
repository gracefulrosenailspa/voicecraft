Re-voice with a cloud voice

Converts the recording you loaded above into a different voice and keeps your timing and delivery. Your audio is sent to Puter for processing. The result is a preset voice speaking your performance, not a copy of your own voice.

Clone your voice

Trains a private voice from the recording you loaded above, then selects it under Pro keys in Narrate. Use one clear speaker with no music, at least 10 seconds. A minute or two sounds better.

Creating a clone uploads this recording to the selected provider. Fish Audio relay uploads are limited to 25 MiB.

Two different things. The effects and Re-voice change a recording you already have. Cloning trains a voice at Fish Audio or ElevenLabs from your sample, and that voice can then read any new text. Their plans decide whether cloning is included, and ElevenLabs lists instant cloning from its Starter plan.
Device voices run on your phone or computer. Cloud narration sends your text to the provider you select. Re-voice uploads your recording to Puter; voice cloning uploads it to Fish Audio or ElevenLabs.
